# Hand Hygiene Guide

## Why Hand Hygiene is Important
Hand hygiene is the single most important practice to prevent the spread of infections. Proper handwashing can reduce respiratory infections by 16-21% and diarrheal illnesses by up to 40%.

## When to Wash Your Hands
- Before eating or preparing food
- After using the toilet
- After coughing, sneezing, or blowing your nose
- After touching animals or animal waste
- After handling garbage
- Before and after caring for someone who is sick
- After touching public surfaces (doorknobs, handrails, elevator buttons)

## How to Wash Your Hands Properly
1. Wet your hands with clean, running water (warm or cold)
2. Apply soap and lather well
3. Scrub all surfaces for at least 20 seconds (sing "Happy Birthday" twice)
4. Pay special attention to: between fingers, under nails, backs of hands, thumbs
5. Rinse thoroughly under clean running water
6. Dry with a clean towel or air dry

## Using Hand Sanitizer
When soap and water are not available:
- Use a hand sanitizer with at least 60% alcohol
- Apply to the palm of one hand
- Rub hands together covering all surfaces
- Continue rubbing until hands feel dry (about 20 seconds)
- Hand sanitizer does not eliminate all types of germs

## When Hand Sanitizer is NOT Enough
- When hands are visibly dirty or greasy
- After using the toilet
- Before preparing food
- After touching chemicals
- When caring for someone with infectious disease

## Common Handwashing Mistakes
- Not washing long enough (need at least 20 seconds)
- Skipping areas like between fingers and thumbs
- Not drying hands properly (wet hands transfer germs more easily)
- Using dirty towels to dry hands
- Touching the faucet with clean hands after washing
