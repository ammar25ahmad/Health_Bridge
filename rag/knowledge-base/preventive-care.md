# Preventive Healthcare Guide

## What is Preventive Healthcare?
Preventive healthcare focuses on maintaining health and preventing diseases before they occur, rather than treating them after they develop. It is more effective and less costly than treating illnesses.

## Types of Preventive Care

### Primary Prevention (Preventing Disease Before It Occurs)
- Vaccinations and immunizations
- Healthy lifestyle choices (diet, exercise, sleep)
- Avoiding tobacco and excessive alcohol
- Regular physical activity (150 minutes per week)
- Maintaining a healthy weight

### Secondary Prevention (Early Detection)
- Regular health screenings and check-ups
- Blood pressure monitoring (at least every 2 years)
- Cholesterol screening (every 4-6 years for adults over 20)
- Diabetes screening (every 3 years for adults over 35)
- Cancer screenings (mammograms, colonoscopies, Pap smears)
- Vision and hearing tests

### Tertiary Prevention (Managing Existing Conditions)
- Regular follow-up appointments
- Medication management for chronic conditions
- Rehabilitation programs
- Disease management education

## Recommended Health Screenings by Age

### Adults 18-39
- Blood pressure: Every 2 years
- Cholesterol: Every 4-6 years
- Diabetes: Every 3 years (starting at 35, or earlier if overweight)
- Skin checks: Annual self-examination

### Adults 40-64
- All screenings from the 18-39 group
- Eye exams: Every 2-4 years
- Mammograms: Every 1-2 years for women 50-74
- Colonoscopy: Every 10 years starting at 50
- Prostate screening: Discuss with doctor

### Adults 65+
- All previous screenings
- Bone density test: For women over 65
- Hearing and vision checks: Annual
- Flu vaccine: Annual
- Pneumonia vaccine: As recommended

## Preventive Health Habits
1. Get regular check-ups even when you feel healthy
2. Stay up to date on vaccinations
3. Maintain a healthy weight (BMI 18.5-24.9)
4. Exercise regularly
5. Don't smoke - quitting at any age helps
6. Limit alcohol to moderate amounts
7. Manage stress through healthy activities
8. Get 7-9 hours of sleep per night
9. Wear seatbelts and practice safety
10. Maintain good dental hygiene
