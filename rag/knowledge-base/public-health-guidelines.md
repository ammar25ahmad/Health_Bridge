# Public Health Guidelines

## What is Public Health?
Public health is the science of protecting and improving the health of communities through education, policy, and research for disease prevention and health promotion.

## Key Public Health Principles

### Disease Prevention
- Stay up to date on recommended vaccinations
- Practice good hand hygiene
- Follow food safety guidelines
- Use safe water sources
- Practice safe sex to prevent sexually transmitted infections

### Health Education
- Learn about common health conditions and their prevention
- Understand the importance of regular health screenings
- Know when to seek medical attention
- Share accurate health information with family and community

### Community Health
- Support community health programs and initiatives
- Participate in local health screenings and events
- Volunteer for health-related causes
- Advocate for policies that promote community health

## Common Public Health Programs
- **Immunization Programs**: Free or low-cost vaccines for children and adults
- **Nutrition Assistance**: Programs like WIC and food banks
- **Maternal and Child Health**: Prenatal care, newborn screening, and pediatric services
- **Tobacco Cessation**: Programs to help people quit smoking
- **Mental Health Services**: Community counseling and support groups
- **Environmental Health**: Water quality, air quality, and food safety inspections

## Health Emergency Preparedness
- Keep a basic first aid kit at home and in your car
- Know the location of your nearest emergency room
- Have emergency contact numbers readily available
- Keep a supply of essential medications
- Know your blood type and any allergies
- Have copies of important medical documents

## When to Seek Professional Help
- Symptoms persist for more than a few days
- Symptoms worsen over time
- You experience sudden severe symptoms
- Over-the-counter treatments are not working
- You need mental health support
- You have questions about medications or treatments
