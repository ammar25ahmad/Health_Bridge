# Nutrition Guide

## What is Good Nutrition?
Good nutrition means getting the right amount of nutrients from a variety of foods to maintain health, energy, and well-being. A balanced diet provides carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, and minerals your body needs.

## Key Principles of Healthy Eating
1. Eat a variety of foods from all food groups daily
2. Choose whole grains over refined grains
3. Include fruits and vegetables in every meal
4. Choose lean proteins like fish, chicken, beans, and nuts
5. Limit processed foods, added sugars, and excessive salt
6. Stay hydrated with water as your primary beverage

## Recommended Daily Intake
- **Fruits and vegetables**: At least 5 servings per day
- **Water**: 6-8 glasses per day
- **Fiber**: 25-30 grams per day
- **Sodium**: Less than 2,300 mg per day
- **Added sugars**: Less than 25 grams per day for women, 36 grams for men

## Food Groups
- **Fruits and Vegetables**: Rich in vitamins, minerals, and fiber
- **Whole Grains**: Provide sustained energy and fiber
- **Lean Proteins**: Build and repair tissues, support immune function
- **Dairy or Alternatives**: Support bone health with calcium and vitamin D
- **Healthy Fats**: Support brain function and cell growth

## Practical Tips for Better Nutrition
- Plan your meals ahead of time to avoid unhealthy choices
- Cook at home more often to control ingredients and portions
- Read nutrition labels when buying packaged foods
- Don't skip breakfast - it fuels your body for the day
- Practice portion control using smaller plates
- Keep healthy snacks accessible (fruits, nuts, yogurt)

## Nutrition Myths to Avoid
- Skipping meals does not help with weight management
- All fats are not bad - healthy fats from nuts and fish are essential
- Carbohydrates are not the enemy - whole grains are healthy
- Supplements cannot replace a balanced diet
