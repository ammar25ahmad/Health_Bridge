# First Aid Basics

## What is First Aid?
First aid is the immediate care given to a person who has been injured or suddenly become ill. It helps maintain life, prevent the condition from worsening, and promote recovery.

## Essential First Aid Skills

### For Minor Cuts and Wounds
1. Wash your hands before treating the wound
2. Stop bleeding by applying gentle pressure with a clean cloth
3. Clean the wound gently with clean running water
4. Apply antibiotic ointment if available
5. Cover with a sterile bandage or adhesive bandage
6. Change the bandage daily and keep the wound clean

### For Burns
1. Cool the burn immediately under cool (not cold) running water for 10-20 minutes
2. Do NOT apply ice, butter, or toothpaste
3. Remove jewelry or tight clothing near the burn
4. Cover loosely with a sterile, non-stick bandage
5. Take over-the-counter pain relief if needed
6. Seek medical help for burns larger than your palm or on face/joints

### For Choking (Adults)
1. Ask the person if they are choking
2. If they cannot speak, cough, or breathe:
   - Stand behind the person
   - Make a fist with one hand, place it above the navel
   - Grasp your fist with the other hand
   - Give quick, upward thrusts (Heimlich maneuver)
3. Repeat until the object is expelled or the person becomes unconscious
4. Call emergency services immediately

### For Severe Bleeding
1. Apply firm, direct pressure with a clean cloth or gauze
2. Maintain continuous pressure for at least 10 minutes
3. If possible, elevate the injured area above the heart
4. Do NOT remove the cloth if it soaks through - add more on top
5. Call emergency services

### For Allergic Reactions
1. Help the person use their epinephrine auto-injector (EpiPen) if available
2. Call emergency services immediately
3. Help the person lie down with legs elevated
4. Loosen tight clothing
5. Stay with the person until help arrives

## When to Call Emergency Services (911)
- Person is unconscious or not breathing
- Severe bleeding that won't stop
- Suspected broken bones or spinal injury
- Chest pain or difficulty breathing
- Severe allergic reaction
- Seizures
- Suspected poisoning
- Burns covering large areas

## Important Principles
- Stay calm and assess the situation
- Ensure your own safety first
- Call for help before starting first aid
- Do not move the injured person unless they are in immediate danger
- First aid is temporary - always seek professional medical help
