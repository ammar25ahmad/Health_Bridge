# Healthy Lifestyle Guide

## What is a Healthy Lifestyle?
A healthy lifestyle is a way of living that reduces the risk of serious illness and premature death. It is built on consistent daily habits that support physical and mental well-being.

## Physical Activity
- Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate aerobic activity per week
- Include strength training exercises at least 2 days per week
- Examples: brisk walking, cycling, swimming, dancing
- Take movement breaks if you have a sedentary job
- Start slowly and gradually increase intensity
- Find activities you enjoy to stay motivated

## Sleep
- Adults need 7-9 hours of sleep per night
- Maintain a consistent sleep schedule (same bedtime and wake time)
- Create a dark, quiet, cool sleeping environment
- Avoid screens (phones, computers, TV) 1 hour before bedtime
- Limit caffeine after 2 PM
- Avoid heavy meals before sleep
- Good sleep improves concentration, productivity, and immune function

## Stress Management
- Practice mindfulness or meditation (even 10 minutes daily helps)
- Maintain social connections with family and friends
- Set realistic goals and priorities
- Take regular breaks during work
- Engage in hobbies and relaxation activities
- Spend time in nature
- Practice deep breathing exercises
- Learn to say no to avoid overcommitment

## Mental Health
- Recognize the signs of stress, anxiety, and depression
- Talk about your feelings with trusted people
- Seek professional help when needed - it is a sign of strength
- Stay connected with others
- Maintain a sense of purpose
- Practice gratitude daily

## Harmful Habits to Avoid
- **Tobacco**: Smoking is the leading cause of preventable death
- **Excessive alcohol**: Limit to moderate amounts (1 drink/day women, 2/day men)
- **Sedentary lifestyle**: Sitting for prolonged periods increases health risks
- **Poor diet**: Processed foods and excess sugar contribute to chronic disease
- **Sleep deprivation**: Chronic lack of sleep affects all body systems

## Building Healthy Habits
- Start with small, achievable changes
- Focus on one habit at a time
- Track your progress
- Don't be discouraged by setbacks
- Celebrate small victories
- Build a support system
- Make healthy choices the easy choices
